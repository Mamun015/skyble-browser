New
- The browser checks for a new version every time it opens, and once an hour
  while it stays open. The update popup comes back after a restart.
- A finished download shows a small bar with an Open button, and the download
  notification has an Open action too.
- When the browser's own update finishes downloading, the install screen opens
  by itself.

Fixed

  Both limits are gone.
- YouTube downloads now record the file and offer Open like other downloads.
