Skyble Browser is a small, fast web browser for Android. It has no ads, no
tracking, no account, and no advertising SDK. About 2.9 MB of the 3 MB download
is the video player engine; pages are drawn by the Android System WebView that
is already on your phone instead of a second copy of Chromium.

Tabs

Open up to 50 tabs. The tab screen shows them as a grid of square cards with a
picture of each page, so you can tell them apart at a glance, and your open tabs
come back when you start the app again.

Video player

Videos from websites and videos you downloaded play in the app's own player:
MP4, WebM, MKV, MOV, MP3, M4A, HLS and DASH. Swipe up or down on the left half
for brightness, on the right half for volume, and sideways to seek. Fit, Fill
and Stretch ratios, 0.5x to 2x speed, audio track choice, mute, and hardware or
software decoding that can be switched while a video plays.

Downloads

YouTube videos save at up to 1080p as ordinary MP4 files; high qualities arrive
as separate picture and sound tracks and are joined on the phone. Media from
other sites, plus PDF, ZIP and APK links, lands in the same built-in list with
progress, speed and a cancel button. When a download starts you can choose the
built-in downloader or a download manager already on the phone, such as ADM,
and remember that choice.

Privacy

Skyble blocks known advertising and tracking hosts, including doubleclick,
googleadservices, taboola, outbrain, adsterra and several others. There is no
analytics or telemetry of any kind. History, bookmarks, cookies, cache and
everything else live on the phone and can be cleared from the settings screen
in one tap. If a site presents a broken security certificate, Skyble warns you
before the page opens and lets you decide.

Reading

Choose a light theme, a dark theme, follow the system setting, or force every
web page dark. Pages that ship their own dark theme keep it. Text size can be
set from 85% to 130%, and images or JavaScript can be switched off to save
data.

Language

The interface is English by default, with a complete Bengali translation
available in the settings. Pages written in English can be translated to
Bengali from the AI section, off by default.

Updates

Skyble can check GitHub releases for a newer version and open the download for
you.
