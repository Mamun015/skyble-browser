# Skyble Browser brand policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This file explains how the Skyble name and artwork may be used. It applies to
everyone who downloads the app, reads the source, forks the project or publishes
a build.

## What the brand is

The following are brand assets of the Skyble Browser project, owned by its
author:

- The name **Skyble**
- The names **Skyble Browser** and **Skyble Browser for Android**
- The Skyble logo and the launcher icon (`res/mipmap-*`, `assets/icon.png`)
- The home screen logo and the README banner (`docs/banner.png`)
- The Android package name `com.skyble.browser`

The code is open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. **The brand assets
are not part of that license.** They are all rights reserved.

## What you may do without asking

- Download, install and use the app.
- Link to this repository or to a release, and quote its name and icon in an
  article, video, review or app directory listing that points to the official
  download.
- Share unmodified APKs from this repository's releases.
- Fork the code, change it, publish it and sell support for it, as long as you
  follow the MPL-2.0 and the next section.

## What you may not do

- Publish a modified build under the name Skyble or Skyble Browser, or under a
  name that is confusingly similar.
- Use the Skyble logo, icon or banner as the icon of your own app, browser,
  website, extension, package, repository or app store listing.
- Remove or hide the trademark notice, then redistribute the app as if it were
  official.
- Register a domain, social account, package name or app store listing that
  suggests you are the Skyble project or its author.
- Claim endorsement, partnership or authorship of Skyble Browser.

## Forks

A fork is welcome. A fork that ships to users must:

1. Replace the app name, the launcher icon, the home screen logo and the banner
   with its own.
2. Change the package name, so the two apps can be installed side by side.
3. Keep the MPL-2.0 license and the copyright notice in `NOTICE`.
4. State plainly, for example "based on Skyble Browser", if it wants to mention
   where the code came from. Mentioning the origin in a sentence is fine; using
   the brand as the product identity is not.

## Registration

The Skyble marks are **not registered trademarks yet**. The author intends to
register them. Registration is what turns these rules into an enforceable
statutory right in most countries; until then, protection rests on copyright in
the artwork, the MPL, and rules against passing off or unfair competition.

If you plan to build a product on this code and want certainty about the name in
your market, register your own brand rather than using this one.

## Reporting misuse

If you find a build, listing or account that uses the Skyble name or artwork
without permission, open an issue on this repository with a link. The project
will ask the platform to remove the copy and, once the marks are registered, will
use the registration.

## Permission

Want to use the name or artwork for something not listed above? Open an issue and
ask. Small, clear requests (a review site, a mirror, a school project) are
usually approved quickly.
